Weekly report — week ending Thu 8 Oct 2026

Tiny Knightfall · source: GA4 export tiny-knightfall-idle-rpg-game.analytics_538412813 · weeks run Friday to Thursday · revenue is gross (VAT-inclusive outside the US, before refunds) · per-version tables cover 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-08 and exclude China unless stated · predecessor: week ending 1 Oct.

An uneventful week. Total revenue $788, up +11% on last week's $710; DAU 520 (was 560); new installs outside China 852 (was 926). Nothing broke, and no new issue appeared in the data. The two things worth knowing are both corrections rather than events:

The apparent jump in ad performance is one account. Ad revenue $174 against $113, eCPM $80 against $57 — but a single new player in Romania is credited $15.00 of it, all three of their impressions logged at exactly $5.00, which are the only $5.00 impressions in the entire export. Removing that one account, eCPM is $73 against $53 — roughly flat. Ad revenue net of the $5.00 impressions is still up +53% and the increase runs across the distribution, not just the top of it, so the ad business did improve; it is the size of the eCPM jump that the three impressions manufacture. User-days carrying an impression worth $1 or more are 15 of 2186 impressions but 36% of ad revenue, up from 18% the week before.

Day-1 retention recovered and the v34 question from last week is closed. On the same six install days, D1 is 26.1% for 02–07 Oct installs against 20.9% for 25–30 Sep. v33 and v34 were never really competing for the same installs — v33 took 8 installs this week (3 of them outside China) — so last week's "v34 progresses less than v33" reading was a rollout-cohort artefact, not a build regression. v34's own first-day progression is stable (42.2% → 40.3% reaching stage 10).

The state of last week's open items is in section 1; the rest of the page is the evidence for the numbers above. Interstitials are unchanged and still by design: 725 INTER impressions exist in the whole export, every one of them an interstitial_backup_<placement> fallback; this week v34 logged 0 regular interstitials. This is not a defect.
