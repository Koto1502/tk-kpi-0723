Week 16–22 Nov: revenue, UA spend, v43 first read

Tiny Knight Fall · 23 Nov 2026 · Android · excludes China · GA4 export through 22 Nov (23 Nov is a partial day and is left out) · orders checked against Google Play Console · UA spend from AppsFlyer

The short version

  1. Revenue fell by two thirds. $229 against $668 the week before: IAP $158 against $577, ads $70 against $90. Google Play Console records 17 orders against 75, so the fall is in actual sales, not in tracking.
  2. Far fewer players paid. 9 buyers against 26. Of last week's 26 buyers, 15 did not play this week, and the 11 who did made one purchase between them. Last week's five largest buyers alone spent $304, 53% of that week's IAP.
  3. UA spend halved, and the traffic moved to lower-value markets. GA ROAS Global 02/11 spent $698 against $1,410 and brought 508 paid installs against 773. All installs fell 25% (1,239 → 926); the Tier-1 share of installs fell from 25% to 17%, and US installs halved (107 → 51).
  4. Daily players fell 17% (474 → 392 a day). Players watched more rewarded ads, but fewer of the views came from Tier-1 countries (26% → 15%), so ad revenue still fell 22%.
  5. v43 is now the live build, on 65% of daily players on 16 Nov and 79% on 22 Nov. New-player retention is level with v39: D1 18.8% against 18.6%, D3 5.5% against 6.8%, both inside the margin of error. Three-day revenue per install is $0.20 against $0.39, from 3 payers against 15; the interval on the difference spans zero.
  6. Build 1 (v39) at day 7: no change in retention. D7 3.0% against 2.1% in the same campaign, inside the margin of error. Mine, Research and Pom Growth still reach about twice as many installs within 7 days; Honey War and Relic still do not (2.2% against 2.6%).
  7. Fishing event, first full week: 46 players. That is 3.5% of v43's weekly players, and 25% of those who played on 3 days or more.
  8. v44, the Arena (PvP) test build, ran on 1–4 test devices a day. No released build contains PvP.

2. Why IAP fell: who bought

The players who paid last week mostly did not come back, and few new buyers replaced them. Revenue at this scale rests on a handful of players: last week's top buyer spent $130 alone, more than the whole IAP of 17–22 Nov.

Buyers9–15 Nov16–22 Nov

Shop interest held up, purchases did not. About as many players opened a shop offer as the week before (), but far fewer of them bought. Two thirds of this week's clicks came from Indonesia, the Philippines and Vietnam, where players buy less often. The event data has no checkout-failure event, so it cannot show whether any purchase attempts failed. The purchases that did go through all appear in Play Console.

3. Traffic: UA spend and where installs came from

The one live campaign spent half as much, and the installs it bought were cheaper and from lower-value markets. Spend on GA ROAS Global 02/11 went from about $200 a day (9–15 Nov) to $57–109 a day (19–22 Nov). Its cost per paid install fell from $1.82 to $1.37 as the mix moved away from Tier-1 countries. A Tier-3 campaign (GA ROAS Tier3 20/11) started on 20 Nov.

8. What these numbers can and cannot say

  • The build change and the traffic change happened together. v43 took over on 15–16 Nov, and UA spend fell from 17 Nov. This week's data cannot split the drop in revenue and daily players between them. The same-campaign comparison in section 5 is the closest thing to a clean read on the build.
  • IAP is a handful of players. 26 buyers last week and 9 this week; one player can move the weekly total by $100 or more.
  • Failed purchases are not logged. The data shows shop clicks and completed purchases, not checkout errors or cancellations.
  • v43 is a week old. D7 for its first full week of installs (16–22 Nov) completes on 29 Nov; its revenue per install needs two to three weeks.
  • AppsFlyer spend for the last days can still change as late cost data arrives.

Method and sources

  • GA4 export (BigQuery), Android, China excluded, 26 Oct–22 Nov. 23 Nov is a partial day and is left out.
  • Revenue: IAP is in_app_purchase value in USD (gross); ads are ad_impression_MAX value. Orders reconciled with the Google Play Console sales report (charged orders by order date).
  • Tier 1: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, Austria, the Nordic countries, Japan and South Korea.
  • Version per day: the version a player logged most events on that day. Install build: the version on the player's first_open.
  • Campaign: GA4 first-touch campaign of the install. UA spend and paid installs: AppsFlyer daily report.
  • Retention day N = any event on calendar day N after install. Feature first use is defined as in the 9–15 Nov report.
  • Intervals: Wilson 95% for shares, normal approximation for differences in shares, bootstrap for revenue.
