6 · Conclusion¶

This week (09-25 … 10-01): $710 total — $597 IAP + $113 ads — vs ~$1,445/week in the two pre-removal weeks (−51%). DAU 560 (527 ex-China) vs 861; installs 926 ex-China vs ~1,820; payers 37 vs ~62.

1 · No-ads sale removal (v32, live 09-21)¶

  • Store level: the .sale packs were $300–320/week; they are now $0. All no-ads revenue fell $514 → $131/week. Players still look for the offer: no-ads card taps per 1k DAU went 10–12 → 23, and per new install 2.3–3.3× — they see $29.99 and mostly leave.
  • Per new install (ex-China), the loss is partly made up elsewhere. No-ads $ per install is down $0.14–0.16 (significant in every window). Other IAP (+$0.05…+0.14) and ads (+$0.03) moved up, not significantly. Net, excluding the single top payer in each arm: −$0.07 to −$0.09 per install (≈ −13 to −25%) in every window D0 … D0–D6; with the top payer the D0–D6 net is +$0.04. Every interval on the net includes zero.
  • Read: a real loss of roughly $65–85/week at current install volume — much smaller than the $310/week of .sale revenue that disappeared, because some of those buyers now spend elsewhere. It is not the main reason revenue halved (see 4). If a discounted no-ads offer comes back, fix the refund/non-delivery issue on permanent.sale first (memory: most-refunded SKU, 7 of 20 refunds).

2 · China¶

  • 635 China installs in the 09-18 week (297 on 09-19 alone), 98–100% on the old v29 build — a build no longer distributed on Play, so this is almost certainly a third-party Android store re-hosting an APK. Now down to 5–20/day.
  • They are real players but cannot monetise: ~28 min played per user (rest: 30), but $0 IAP and $0.12 of ads from 891 users. Rewarded fill is 28% and they saw 0.02 impressions each — Google Play billing and the ad networks are not reachable there. D1 18% / D7 1.9% (rest 25.6 / 4.5).
  • Impact: +136 DAU/day in the 09-18 week, diluting IAP ARPDAU from 0.196 to 0.162; no revenue effect either way. Report KPIs ex-China. If the APK is pirated, ask whether to block it — it costs server load and nothing else.

3 · Why ad revenue is low (~$113/week, 14% of revenue)¶

  • It is not price. eCPM is healthy and rising ($48 → $57), and ad $ per DAU actually doubled (0.016 → 0.031) as the base shrank to its engaged core.
  • It is reach. Ads are 100% opt-in rewarded — the game has no regular interstitials (all 214 interstitial impressions in 4 weeks are the interstitial_backup_* fallback). Only 8.6% of DAU watch any ad on a given day, ~6 ads each → 0.54 impressions per DAU. acquire_hero alone is 37% of ad revenue.
  • 20% of ad rewards are paid without an impression: 10,793 ad_reward vs 8,670 rewarded impressions in 4 weeks; 404 users got 2,656 rewards and never saw a single ad (one user: 431). Not explained by no-ads buyers (19 users, no gap). At current eCPM that is ~$55/week (+50% of ad revenue) if those were real views — dev check: is there a skip-ad perk (membership/VIP) or an exploit?
  • Loading has improved: rewarded fill 51% → 71%, and AppLovin DNS-fail retries fell 65k → 10k/week.
  • Levers, in order: (1) find the reward-without-ad leak; (2) more rewarded placements on high-intent moments like acquire_hero / BossRush (eCPM $82–87); (3) consider a capped interstitial for non-payers — the only way to move the 91% who never opt in.

4 · IAP¶

  • The $726/week drop = −$383 no-ads + −$343 other IAP. Of the other-IAP drop, $311 is just fewer players (DAU ex-China −38%) and only $33 is lower spend per player (0.135 → 0.126 per DAU).
  • So the main driver is the shrinking install base: UA volume roughly halved — the two FB campaigns and GA_…ROAS_Tier1_29082026 ended, and the new …Tier1_29092026 is small (34 installs). Not whale-driven: the top payer is 7–13% of each week.

5 · Retention¶

  • D1 fell to 20.9% for 09-25 week installs, vs 24.6–26.6% for the seven weeks before; D3 8.8% vs ~9.8%. D7 was stable (4.7–6.7%) through the 09-18 cohort.
  • It is not traffic mix — D1 fell inside every campaign: ROAS Global 34 → 24%, Tier1_150926 35.7 → 23.1%, unattributed 20 → 16%, Organic 25.6 → 24.1%. It coincides with v34 becoming the install build (09-25). ~690 eligible installs, so this needs another week — but check v34 for an onboarding / crash / first-session regression now.

Data quality¶

  • ~18 h with almost no events, 09-28 09:00 → 09-29 03:00 UTC. Ask devs whether the game server was down; otherwise it is an export gap and 09-28 revenue is missing from GA4 (compare Play Console). All figures are gross, VAT-inclusive outside the US, with no refunds.
