Week 9–15 Nov: versions, Build 1 changes and impact

Tiny Knight Fall · 15 Nov 2026 · Android · excludes China · GA4 export through 14 Nov (15 Nov is a partial day) · builds read from the Google Play release

The short version

  1. Five builds ran this week; two reached players. v39 (Build 1, live since 6 Nov) carried 73–84% of daily players from 9 to 14 Nov. v40, v41 and v42 were internal test builds (8 testers a day at most). v43 began rolling out on 14 Nov and was 35% of players on 15 Nov, a partial day.
  2. Three of Build 1's five early unlocks reach almost twice as many new players. Within 3 days of install, Mine use rose from 10.7% to 19.6% of installs, Research from 10.2% to 17.7% and Pom Growth from 5.4% to 10.1%.
  3. Honey War and Relic have not moved yet. The build sets both to stage 26, but players still first use them much later: 2.1% of installs within 3 days, against 2.8% before, and 3 of the 83 new players between stages 30 and 63.
  4. Retention is unchanged so far. D1 18.6% against 19.6% and D3 6.8% against 7.4%; both differences are inside the margin of error. D7 can be read from 21 Nov.
  5. Revenue per install is not yet readable. $0.39 against $0.24 over the first 3 days, from 15 and 6 payers; the interval on the difference spans zero.
  6. Hero tracking is live. 315 hero pick-ups are logged with hero and source. 9.8% of new installs received Faun from Cat Guide and 5.0% earned Volt from ads.
  7. No released build contains PvP. v43 adds the TikTok SDK and the Fishing event screens and keeps Build 1's unlock stages.

1. Versions run this week

v39 was the live build all week; v43 started replacing it on 14 Nov. Daily players by the version they ran most that day. Daily players went from about 650 (4–7 Nov) to 410–480 (10–14 Nov) while daily installs went from 210–400 to 115–205; retention per install did not change (section 3c).

3. Impact of Build 1 on new players

How the comparison is made. A new UA campaign (GA ROAS Global 02/11) started four days before Build 1, so the two builds are compared inside that one campaign: v37 installs of 2–5 Nov against v39 installs of 6–13 Nov. Three-day figures use v37 installs of 2–3 Nov (391) and v39 installs of 6–11 Nov (1,048), so that no v37 player could have updated inside the window.

4. Heroes: first tracked numbers

Hero pick-ups are now measured instead of estimated. Among v39 installs of 6–11 Nov, 12.1% received at least one hero beyond the starter: 9.8% Faun from Cat Guide and 5.0% Volt from the ad reward. 26.0% of those installs cleared stage 11 within 3 days; Cat Guide opens at stage 11.

5. What these numbers can and cannot say

  • Combined effect only. The five unlock changes, the free hero and the tracking shipped in one build, so retention and revenue cannot be split between them.
  • Short comparison window. The v37 group inside the same campaign is four install days (Mon–Thu); the v39 group is eight. Day-of-week mix differs slightly.
  • D7 and later are not readable yet. D7 for v39 installs of 6–13 Nov completes on 20 Nov. The first D1 figure for v43 completes on 16 Nov.
  • Revenue is driven by a handful of payers. At about 1.4% of installs paying in the first 3 days, one large purchase moves the average.
  • Feature use is read from events. A feature counts as used at the first event that only it produces; a player who opened a feature without acting in it is not counted.

Method and sources

  • GA4 export (BigQuery), Android, China excluded, 26 Oct–15 Nov; 14 Nov is the last complete day.
  • Version per day: the version a player logged most events on that day. Install build: the version on the player's first_open.
  • Campaign: GA4 first-touch campaign of the install. Old campaign = GA ROAS Global 20/07; same campaign = GA ROAS Global 02/11.
  • Stage cleared = level_win level − 1. Retention day N = any event on calendar day N after install.
  • Intervals: Wilson 95% for shares, normal approximation for differences in shares, bootstrap for revenue.
  • Build contents: unlock table and code read from the Google Play releases of v37 (26 Oct), v39 (14 Nov) and v43 (15 Nov).
