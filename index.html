Read these numbers with care

  • Revenue is gross. The GA4 export carries no refund or cancellation event, so every revenue figure here is before refunds. Reconcile against Play Console before treating one as booked.
  • Revenue tracking starts 2026-07-20 (app version 16), which is a tracking boundary, not the store opening — purchase events fire on real SKUs from 2026-07-07. "No payers before 07-20" is likely an instrumentation artifact.
  • Campaign attribution is partial. Roughly 45% of installs resolve to a campaign across the whole base (rising to ~85% among players who reach campaign stages); the rest show as "(unattributed)". Filtering to one campaign samples the players, it never partitions them.
  • Revenue is whale-dominated at this scale. A single player can be a double-digit share of a country or campaign total. Check concentration before believing a per-country number.
  • Don't pool the Tier 1 campaigns. Their conversion rates differ by more than 20×, so pooling drags every rate toward the cheaper campaign's floor.
