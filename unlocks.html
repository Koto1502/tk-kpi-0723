Track 1 — campaign stage feature unlocks hang off the trunk at their gate; the curve below is the share reaching that stage, same axis

Axis is square-root scaled so the crowded early gates (10–31) get room. Red strip = share of clears with at least one loss on that stage, per build — the walls.

Track 2 — main quest same layout: gates on the trunk, share reaching that quest number below

Main quests run in parallel with the campaign: most players who play claim quests 1–42 on their first day, so this track decays far less than the campaign one.

Every unlock share of installs that reached the gate — current filters and horizon

How to read this
  • Gates come from the game's own FeatureUnlockData config (41 entries, datamined 2026-10-26): campaign stage, main-quest number, or mine depth. Mine depth (Hero Enhance, Mastery at depth 40) is not tracked in the event data, so it is listed but not measured.
  • Reached a stage = cleared it (level_win reports the stage advanced into, so stage N is cleared at level_win(N+1)). Reached a main quest = claimed that many main-quest rewards (each claim counted once).
  • Within day N counts only installs observed for at least N whole days; players who installed before the 10-18 → 10-20 export gap are followed only to 10-17, installs on gap days are dropped. That is why new builds have few or no Day 7 / Day 14 installs.
  • Of all installs includes players who quit in seconds (many never start stage 1); of players who started the track divides by those who cleared stage 1 / claimed quest 1, to compare progression without the first-minute drop.
  • Build = the build the player installed on (they may update later). Builds with fewer than 150 installs are grouped as “other”. Android only, China excluded. Campaign from AppsFlyer; most players who quit in the first seconds have no AppsFlyer ID, so “Unattributed” is mostly them.
